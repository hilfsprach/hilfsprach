About Us

A Small Idea with Serious Intentions

Hilfsprach was founded in 2022 by Angalo Hentich T, with just one laptop and a strong belief: anyone can learn any language with the right guidance, environment, and support.

What began as a small idea has grown into a dedicated language-learning community helping learners across India and around the world learn languages with confidence.

Today, Hilfsprach offers German, English, French, Japanese, and Russian language training, both online and offline, with a strong focus on practical communication and personalized learning.

Learning in Small Batches. Growing with Confidence.

At Hilfsprach, quality comes before quantity. We intentionally keep our batches to a maximum of 8 students to ensure that every learner receives the attention and guidance they deserve.

Small batches give our students more opportunities to speak, participate, ask questions, receive personalized feedback, and build confidence throughout their learning journey.

Certified Trainers. Practical Learning.

Our certified, friendly, and experienced trainers create a supportive learning environment where students feel comfortable learning, communicating, making mistakes, and improving every day.

Our language courses follow the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) wherever applicable, providing learners with a structured and internationally recognized approach to language proficiency.

But learning a language goes beyond textbooks and grammar. Our classrooms focus on the skills that matter in real life:

Conversation and communication
Active classroom participation
Practical language use
Cultural understanding and integration
Confidence in speaking
Personalized guidance and feedback

More Than a Language Course

At Hilfsprach, we believe that learning a language is not simply about completing a level or passing an examination. It is about gaining the confidence to communicate, connect, study, work, travel, and build meaningful experiences across cultures.

Whether you want to learn German, English, French, Japanese, or Russian, prepare for a language examination, study or work abroad, or simply improve your communication skills, Hilfsprach is here to support you at every step.

From our roots in Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, to learners across India, the UK, the Middle East, and Europe, our mission remains the same: to make quality language education accessible, engaging, and effective for everyone.

Services

More than classrooms — a full pathway.

From your first placement test to your visa interview, online or at our Nagercoil campus.

Attend on campus in Nagercoil or join the same batch live online — one trainer, one class, two doors. Evening slots are tuned to European time (CET), so learners in the UK, the Middle East, Germany and across Europe study with us in real time, with recordings for revision.

A dedicated B1/B2 track for candidates heading to Germany for vocational training or healthcare jobs — language, medical German, certificate preparation and visa-interview coaching in one program.

We register candidates for Goethe, telc, TestDaF, IELTS, JLPT, TORFL and DELF — slots, paperwork and preparation for the exact exam edition you'll sit.

Every Saturday at 10:00 — a 30-minute assessment that maps you to the right CEFR level and the right batch, with no obligation to enrol.

Certified translation of documents between German, English, French, Japanese and Russian — for universities, consulates and employers.

Custom batches for schools, hospitals and companies — on site, online, or hybrid. Foreign-language electives, staff upskilling and exam-ready cohorts.
